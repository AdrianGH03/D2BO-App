# 🎯 D2BO – Destiny 2 Bounty Organizer

**D2BO** is a web application built for **Destiny 2** players who want to organize and optimize their bounty progression. By categorizing and counting overlapping objectives across all your bounties, D2BO helps you **maximize XP gains** and **minimize wasted time**.

🎮 **Live Demo:** [https://d2bo-login.netlify.app](https://d2bo-login.netlify.app)

---

## 👋 Welcome to D2BO!

Destiny 2 is full of bounties — and managing them efficiently can be frustrating. **D2BO** makes this process easy by showing you:

- 📊 Which objectives appear most across your bounties
- 🔄 How to group tasks by activity type (like "Auto Rifle kills", "Crucible matches", etc.)
- 🎁 How to **prioritize** bounties that stack, helping you:
  - Earn **Season Pass XP**
  - Level up your **Seasonal Artifact**
  - Gain **Bright Dust**
  - Knock out **Weekly Challenges** faster

---

## 💡 Why D2BO?

> _“While playing Destiny 2, I was tired of constantly opening my inventory to check my bounties. I wanted a way to quickly see what objectives overlap so I could get more done in less time.”_ — *Miguel Gomez*

Instead of flipping back and forth through menus, D2BO gives you a **clear overview** of what to focus on. Just look for the **top subcategories** with the most overlapping bounties — and go!

---

## 🚀 Key Features

- 🧾 View a breakdown of **active bounties** by objective type  
- 📈 See which **subcategories** (e.g., weapon types, activities) appear most  
- 🔍 Easily **prioritize tasks** to maximize reward efficiency  
- 💻 Simple, responsive web UI built for quick glance access during gameplay
